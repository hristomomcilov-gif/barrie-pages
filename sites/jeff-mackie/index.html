About the shop

A Bradford family shop

Jeff Mackie started Jeff Mackie Electric Ltd. in 1976 in the Bradford / Holland Marsh area after serving his apprenticeship with Ainsworth Electric Limited of Toronto, with the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers.

His son Jay started an apprenticeship with the company in 1998, completed his Certificate of Qualification in 2002, and later earned his Master Electrician licence so the shop could continue under Ontario’s contractor licensing rules. Jay is now owner/operator and runs the daily work.

  1. 1976 Jeff Mackie opens the shop in Bradford / Holland Marsh.
  2. 1998–2002 Jay apprentices, writes his C. of Q., and returns as a working partner.
  3. 2006 Jay obtains his Master Electrician licence for the ECRA/ESA contractor licence.
  4. 2018 Jay is partner/owner running the daily duties of the company.
