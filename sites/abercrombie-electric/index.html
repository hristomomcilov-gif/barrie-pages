The Shop

Established in 1946

Robert R. Abercrombie established Abercrombie Electric in February of 1946 in Barrie.

Robert Jordan ran the business until he retired in 1999. Rene LeCollier has been President since December 1999. R. Michael is Chief Estimator. R. Matthew is Office Manager.
