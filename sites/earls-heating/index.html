The shop

Personal service from Earl or Brandon

With most heating and air conditioning companies, being serviced by the same technician each time is nearly impossible. Not so here. Earl's Heating is a family business operated by Earl Burke and son Brandon. Each service call is performed professionally and personally by either Earl or Brandon.

We are committed to quality, prompt, qualified service at a reasonable price — from new sales and installation of home comfort products to regular service or repair of most makes and models.

We are a fully certified member of professional HVAC associations, including the Canadian Oil Heat Association. The shop is at 160 Balsam Street in Collingwood.
