The shop

A small family shop

Fitch Electrical Contracting is locally based and family owned. We are a fully licensed and insured contractor. We build the company one customer at a time.

Tim Fitchett is the Master Electrician. With over 10 years of residential and commercial electrical experience, the goal is to solve electrical problems in a timely and cost-efficient manner. No customer is too big or small.

We specialize in home renovation, lighting design, and electrical service for homeowners and businesses in the greater Simcoe County area — from design to completion.
